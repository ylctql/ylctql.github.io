About me

I am a Ph.D. student in quantum information at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University. I am a member of TIQC Lab, advised by Prof. Luming Duan.

My interests center on the physical implementation of quantum computers, with a particular focus on trapped-ion systems. I am also interested in quantum optics, atomic and molecular physics, quantum artificial intelligence, and the use of AI in fundamental scientific research.

I received my bachelor’s degree from Tsinghua University in 2025. At Weiyang College, I studied Mathematics and Physics alongside Engineering Physics.

Selected projects

Sep 2026 – Present · Quantum artificial intelligence

Efficient quantum circuit evaluation

Research with Prof. Dongling Deng · IIIS, Tsinghua University

Developed a transferable quantum circuit evaluator by jointly encoding Hamiltonian and quantum circuit features.

Mar 2026 – Jun 2026 · Trapped-ion quantum computing

Image processing and recognition for large-scale ion crystals

Research with Prof. Luming Duan · IIIS, Tsinghua University

Developed an image-processing algorithm for ion crystals affected by strong crosstalk caused by large-amplitude micromotion. Achieved high-accuracy ion identification in a two-dimensional crystal containing more than 1,700 ions.

Dec 2025 – Jun 2026 · Trapped-ion quantum computing

GPU-accelerated parallel simulation of large-scale ion crystal dynamics

Research with Prof. Luming Duan · IIIS, Tsinghua University

Implemented GPU-accelerated parallel simulations of two-dimensional ion-crystal dynamics for systems of up to 10,000 ions.

Jun 2024 – Jun 2025 · Trapped-ion quantum computing

Design and development of a trapped-ion quantum computing system

Research with Prof. Luming Duan · IIIS, Tsinghua University

Designed and simulated integrated ion traps compatible with PCB fabrication, including a monolithic junction-based trap. Simulated ion-transport dynamics and the dynamics of two-dimensional planar ion crystals as part of a broader effort toward scalable trapped-ion quantum computing.
